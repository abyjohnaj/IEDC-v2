About IEDC BMCE

Located at Baselios Mathews II College of Engineering, Sasthamcotta, Kollam, IEDC creates an ecosystem where engineering curiosity meets market execution.

💡

Ideation & Research

Structured design thinking sessions and ideation hackathons to help students solve real-world industry problems.

🛠️

Prototyping Lab

Access to modern IoT hardware, 3D printing equipment, and maker spaces to build Functional Minimum Viable Products (MVPs).

🚀

Pre-Incubation Support

Mentorship from successful startup founders, patent filing guidance, and seed funding support via Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM).

🌐

Industry Connections

Networking sessions with angel investors, corporate leaders, legal advisors, and technology evangelists.
